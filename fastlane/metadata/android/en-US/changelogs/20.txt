v0.8.3 — the WhatsApp-shaped overhaul.

Chats screen:
• Three-tab bottom navigation (Chats / Community / Settings)
  replaces the old tile-grid home. One thumb to switch surfaces.
• Conversations are full-screen now — bottom nav hides while
  you're in a thread.
• "+" FAB on the Chats tab opens a sheet with "New group" /
  "Pair a new peer".
• Sync banner and pull-to-refresh removed. Background auto-sync
  is silent; a hairline indicator at the top shows when it's
  actively running.

Reply quoting:
• Long-press any message → "Reply". The composer shows a
  "Replying" card with the quoted snippet. Send → the recipient's
  bubble has a quote pill above showing what you replied to.
• Tap a quote pill → list scrolls to the original message.
• Works in 1:1 and group conversations.

Pair a new peer:
• The QR-handshake flow is reachable again post-onboarding.
  Find it from the Chats FAB, the empty-Chats CTA, or
  Settings → Pair a new peer.

Peer details:
• Tap the conversation title → bottom sheet with the peer's full
  fingerprint, Rename, Notes (free-form local notes about the
  peer — never synced), and View web page.

Onion profile page:
• New look: card-on-glow layout, gradient headline, pill-shaped
  link buttons with hover-lift. Still pure HTML+CSS, no
  JavaScript, no fonts loaded, CSP unchanged.

Update banner:
• When a paired peer is running a newer Keystone, a banner
  appears on the Chats tab. Tap → walk-through for asking the
  peer to share their build.

Community tab:
• Flattened into a single searchable list of paired peers with
  trust-level dots. Tap the trust-graph icon (top right) for the
  visualization.

Settings:
• One scrollable list: biometric toggles, My page, Mailbox,
  Pair a new peer, Wallet, Share Keystone, and Reset identity
  (with biometric prompt). No more dead-end "Advanced" link.

Mailbox:
• Purge held messages now requires confirmation.

Behind the scenes:
• Dozens of crypto/transport/messaging bug fixes from the audit
  sweep — see git log for the gory details.
