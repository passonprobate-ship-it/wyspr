v0.7.0a — Monero wallet scaffold.

• New :feature:monero-wallet module wired into the bottom nav.
• All Monero RPC traffic routes through the embedded Tor SOCKS proxy
  (SOCKS5 DOMAINNAME, no DNS leak). Multi-node round-robin across a
  curated public-node pool.
• Wallet UI shows Tor bootstrap status, remote-node chain tip, and a
  clear "engine not bundled" panel. No misleading 0 XMR rows.
• v0.6.6 revocation propagation: revocation certs now flow between
  trusted peers as a piggyback round on the messaging sync.

This is a pre-release alpha. The on-device crypto engine (block scan
+ tx signing) ships in v0.7.0b once the GPL-licensed Monerujo JNI
binding is bundled; balance/history/send remain disabled until then.
