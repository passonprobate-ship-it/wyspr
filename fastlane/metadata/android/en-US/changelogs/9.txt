v0.7.1 — end-to-end encrypted messaging confirmed on hardware.

First release where two paired Keystone devices have demonstrably
exchanged encrypted messages over BLE on physical phones. Prior
releases shipped the wire format but the full Noise XX + sync
exchange had never closed end-to-end.

What changed:
• KeystoreManager.sign() no longer pads the message — every
  signature in the project had been silently failing.
• BleTransport emits the accept Link on GATT CONNECTED so the
  responder side wins the role race deterministically.
• MessageSyncService picks roles from a lexicographic pubkey
  compare (smaller pub dials, larger accepts) — no symmetric
  deadlock.
• BLE write flow control on both client and server: the drainer
  waits for onCharacteristicWrite / onNotificationSent between
  chunks. Without this the Android stack silently dropped any
  Push frame past ~3 chunks.
• Session timeout, stale-Tor drain, MTU clamp, read-receipt
  role split, race-scope detachment, and a half-dozen other
  bugs the prior runs never reached.

UX:
• Tap a contact's fingerprint in a conversation to set a
  friendly name.
• Returning users with at least one trust edge skip the pair
  flow and land in messaging.

Still not hardware-verified: the Tor leg (every successful
round used BLE), revocation propagation, the Monero scaffold,
the vault module.
