v0.8.0 — async mailbox delivery + home menu redesign.

The single biggest feature since messaging itself: a mailbox is a
Keystone device that volunteers to hold encrypted messages for the
community when the recipient is offline. Solves the "both peers must
be online at the same time" failure mode without any central server.

Mailbox highlights:

• New "Mailbox" tile on the home grid. One screen, two roles:
   - Use a mailbox — scan a host's QR; future outbound messages
     auto-route via that mailbox when direct delivery doesn't land.
   - Be a mailbox — toggle Running on; the device starts holding
     sealed envelopes for community peers. Live storage stats. QR
     for peers to scan to point their bindings at you.

• Sealed-box at rest. Every envelope held by a mailbox is wrapped
  with crypto_box_seal to the recipient's X25519 key BEFORE it
  leaves the sender. The mailbox host CAN'T READ messages even
  with physical access to their device's disk.

• Auto-propagation. When you set up a mailbox for yourself, the
  signed binding cert flows to your trust-graph peers via the
  existing sync engine. They learn where to push asynchronous
  mail for you without scanning a separate QR per sender.

• Baked defaults: 50 MB storage cap, 14-day retention, community-
  only senders. No user-facing knobs for v1 — the feature works
  out of the box for everyone in your trust graph.

Other surface polish:

• Home menu redesign. The post-onboarding landing is a clean
  tile grid (Messages, My community, My page, Wallet, Find peers,
  Share app, Mailbox, Settings) — no more wallet-as-front-door.
  Messages tile shows an unread badge.

• "Share app" now reachable from the home (was a hidden side-trip
  inside onboarding for returning users).

• My community screen: fixed an invisible-buttons bug where the
  "View trust graph" + "Back" buttons existed but were squashed to
  0px because the peer list ate all the vertical space.

• Transport robustness: a single transport failing (e.g. BLE off,
  Tor still bootstrapping) no longer aborts the whole sync round.
  Plus richer Tor diagnostic logging.

Two-device proof of the full mailbox round-trip on hardware is the
first item up for the v0.8.1 sprint. The wire format, crypto, and
UI are all in place; this release is the protocol + UI surface.
