v0.8.2 — better-looking photos + tighter bubble + emoji jumbo.

Photos:
• Encoder switched JPEG → WebP. WebP-lossy is ~25–35% more
  efficient at the same visual quality, so the same 16 KB body
  budget now carries either a higher resolution or a higher
  quality factor. Memes (640 px, text-heavy) used to come out
  crunchy at JPEG q≤45; the equivalent WebP at q~65 looks
  substantially cleaner for the same byte count.
• New compression ladder targets 720 / q75 first, falling back
  down to 360 / q45 only if the input refuses to compress.
• Backward-compatible decode: incoming JPEG payloads from v0.8.1
  still render correctly. ImagePayload accepts both magic-byte
  signatures (JPEG SOI + WebP "RIFF…WEBP").
• Photo bubble now wraps tightly to the image instead of sitting
  inside a 78%-wide rectangle with empty space around portrait
  photos. Bubble caps at 300 dp wide, image at 280×360 dp.

Emojis:
• Emoji-only messages (≤ 8 codepoints, no letters/digits) render
  at displaySmall (~42 sp) — the standard "jumbo emoji" pattern
  WhatsApp / iMessage use for reaction-shaped messages.
