v0.8.1 — tiny photos.

You can now send photos inside Keystone threads — 1:1 and groups.

• New photo button in the composer (next to the location pin).
  Tap to open the system photo picker (no extra permissions needed
  on Android 13+; uses the platform photo picker on older versions
  too).
• Adaptive compression. Photos shrink through a ladder of
  (max-dimension, JPEG quality) pairs until the encoded body fits
  the 16 KB message-envelope cap. The output is tiny — typically
  a 240–640 px JPEG between 5–11 KB. Hence "tiny photos."
• Tagged-body encoding (`keystone:img:<base64>`) — same scheme as
  v0.7.6 location messages. Zero wire-format changes, no schema
  bump, the receiver detects the prefix and renders inline.
• Receiver renders the photo as a bubble at max 240 dp tall,
  contentScale=Fit. Broken/malformed payloads fall back to a
  placeholder rather than crashing the thread.

Defaults are aggressive on purpose. The goal is "always
deliverable" over "best looking" — a tiny photo always lands; if
you want pixel quality, that's a future "files" feature with a
chunked envelope.
