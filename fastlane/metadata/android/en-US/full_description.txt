Wyspr is private messaging for people who don't trust the platform layer — no server, no account, no phone number.

Pair in person by scanning QR codes. A Noise XX handshake over Bluetooth establishes a trust edge backed by hardware-keystore identity keys (StrongBox where available, TEE everywhere else). Software-only keys are refused.

Messages are encrypted end-to-end with Noise (X25519 + ChaCha20-Poly1305 + BLAKE2s). Devices reach each other over Bluetooth LE when in range, and over an embedded Tor hidden service across the internet. Each device derives its own stable .onion address from the keystore identity — peers exchange onions during the handshake and can reach each other anywhere afterwards.

Trust is web-of-trust: every authorization decision is evaluated against k-independent paths through signed invitation certificates. A single compromised device cannot impersonate anyone else, and a revoked device is locked out by the next sync round.

Features:
• Encrypted 1:1 and group messaging with read receipts
• Image, voice note, and file sharing
• Reply quoting and emoji reactions
• Disappearing messages (per-conversation timer)
• Message search
• Async mailbox delivery (community members volunteer to hold sealed-box-encrypted envelopes while you're offline)
• Peer-to-peer APK distribution (no app store required)
• Personal .onion profile page (pure HTML, no JS, no tracking)
• Tor bootstrap watchdog (auto-recovers stuck connections)

No telemetry. No analytics. No ads. No metadata logged.
